These two features are easier to use well than the aimbot, and they're also the ones people overconfigure.

## Triggerbot

The triggerbot fires when your crosshair is already on an operator. It never moves your crosshair. You still do the aiming. It just handles the click.

Settings to know:
- **Short delay before the shot.** This is the big one. Instant shots at inhuman speed look odd. A small delay looks like fast reflexes.
- **Head or upper chest.** Same logic as the aim: chest is more forgiving, head is more obvious if it's constant.
- **Same wall check.** It won't fire at someone behind a wall.
- **Hold key.** It only works while you hold the bind, so you control when it's active.

Best for: close-range fights where reaction time matters, and players who like doing the aiming themselves.

## No recoil

On rifles, there's a light settle that takes the edge off recoil. It isn't a perfectly flat laser. That's intentional, because a dead-flat spray over a full magazine stands out in a killcam. There's also a no-spread option.

Best for: players who can aim fine but lose fights because the gun climbs in long bursts.

## Combining them

A comfortable setup for many players:
- Player visuals for information
- Triggerbot on a hold key with a small delay
- Light no recoil on
- Aimbot off or at a tiny circle

You'll still be doing most of the aiming, and your gameplay will look natural.

## Things to avoid

- Zero delay on the triggerbot
- Holding the trigger key permanently
- Running every assist in every lobby
