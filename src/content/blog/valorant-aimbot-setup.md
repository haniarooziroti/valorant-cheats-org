## Think "assist", not "autopilot"
The most common reason players get reported is over-aggressive aim. The goal is to nudge your crosshair, not teleport it.

## Starting values
| Setting | Competitive | Unrated | Deathmatch |
| Aim circle (FOV) | Small | Small–medium | Medium |
| Smoothing | High | High | Medium |
| Target bone | Head only when already near; otherwise chest | Chest/Head mix | Any |
| Aim key | Hold-to-aim | Hold-to-aim | Hold-to-aim |

(Qualitative starting points; tune to your sensitivity and playstyle.)

## Circle size
A smaller circle only helps when you're already close to the target, which is closer to how a skilled player behaves.

## Smoothing
Higher smoothing slows the correction and removes snap. If you can see the crosshair "jump", raise smoothing.

## Bone choice
Head-only is the riskiest. Chest or a mixed bone selection looks more natural and still increases consistency.

## Test before you queue
Use the range or Deathmatch to check how settings feel, then save separate profiles.

## Hold-to-aim and visibility filters
Ranked players on this site typically bind hold-to-aim rather than a always-on toggle. That keeps you manually tracking between fights and only receives correction when you press the key.

Use team and downed-player filters from the aim assist feature list so you are not pulled toward unusable targets during a retake. Distance caps matter on Ascent and Icebox long sightlines—if correction only works inside fifteen meters, your pre-aim still has to do the rest.

## After a patch
Agent updates change movement hitboxes and ability clutter. When Riot ships a note, read the homepage status line before you load ranked, then run one Deathmatch with your ranked profile unchanged. If tracking feels off, adjust smoothing before you touch FOV.

Players in the [killcam tuning thread](/forum/aimbot-settings-smooth-level-killcam/) often drop FOV ten percent after patches instead of raising strength. That thread is discussion, not official defaults—use it to compare notes, not to copy exact numbers without testing.

## Loader vs settings issues
If the menu never opens, that is a loader or license path—email Support with Windows version and error text. If the menu works but aim feels wrong, stay in this guide or the [loader troubleshooting walkthrough](/blog/valorant-loader-fix/) for import order and preset steps.
