There are only two options, and the feature list is identical. The real question is how long you'll keep playing.

## The numbers

Monthly is $35 for 30 days. Lifetime is $150, paid once.

150 divided by 35 is just under 4.3. So if you'd use the tool for five months or more, lifetime is cheaper. At four months, monthly is still slightly cheaper ($140 vs $150). It's a close call right there.

| Months you'll use it | Monthly total | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $35 | $150 |
| 3 | $105 | $150 |
| 4 | $140 | $150 |
| 5 | $175 | $150 |
| 12 | $420 | $150 |

## When monthly is the better choice

- You're trying it for the first time.
- You mostly play during a season and drop the game afterward.
- You want to see how the 6v6 and DMZ setups feel before committing.

## When lifetime is the better choice

- You already know you'll be playing Modern Warfare 4 for months.
- You'd rather not think about renewals.
- You use it daily and want the lowest cost per session.

## A practical way to decide

Buy monthly if you're on the fence. If you're still using it in week three and have your configs dialed in, you'll know lifetime is worth it. Don't overthink it. The difference between the two choices is smaller than the difference between a good and bad setup.
