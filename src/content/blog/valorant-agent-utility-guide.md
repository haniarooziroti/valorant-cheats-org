## ESP is more than wallhack boxes
Valorant rounds are decided by information: where the spike is, where utility landed, who has what. A good overlay surfaces only what you act on.

## Useful overlays (if your product includes them)
- Player outlines/boxes with health
- Spike carrier and plant timers
- Utility indicators (smokes, molly zones, turrets)
- Weapon and credits info for economy reads

## Show less to see more
A screen full of text slows you down. Start with players + spike only, then add one layer at a time.

## Mode considerations
Competitive and Unrated reward subtle information use; Deathmatch is the safest place to test overlays.

## How ESP fits Valorant rounds
Attackers need spike timing and post-plant clarity; defenders care about utility still alive in a stack. When your product exposes carrier tags or plant timers, treat them as reminders—not excuses to wide-swing every angle.

On the homepage feature grid, player ESP is described with corner or full box styles, squad color coding, and distance sliders. Those same controls appear on the module page—use them to keep distant fights off your HUD so you are not reading text during a swing.

## Tuning workflow
1. Enable player outlines with health only.
2. Add spike carrier or plant timer if your build includes it.
3. Layer one utility indicator (smoke zone, molly, or turret) once the first two feel automatic.
4. Save a ranked profile separate from Deathmatch experiments.

If boxes feel noisy on Bind or Haven, lower the distance cap before you change colors. Forum threads on corner box layouts often suggest syncing radar zoom to the same meter value so minimap dots and wall outlines agree.

## Questions players ask here
**Should I run skeleton lines in ranked?** Most write-ups on this site recommend corner boxes with health for ranked readability; skeletons are easier to spot on streams and can clutter your own agent model.

**What if overlays feel like cheating on camera?** Stream-proof options are mentioned on product copy; test a short recording locally before you go live, and dial back pulsing cover colors if they flash on kill cams.
