## What the purchase includes
Access covers the Valorant PC module list described on the product hub—aim assist, player ESP, triggerbot, radar, and no recoil helpers delivered through a loader and license key. Monthly and lifetime windows include the same features; you are choosing how long the key stays active, not a stripped-down tier.

Checkout sends confirmation to the email you type on the payment page. That inbox is where loader access and dashboard login land, so double-check spelling before you submit. If nothing arrives, check spam and promotions folders before you open a support ticket.

## Before ranked: read and test
Walk through the [Windows 10 and 11 setup checklist](/blog/valorant-requirements/) so TPM, Secure Boot, and virtualization match what Vanguard expects. Skipping that step is the fastest way to get a “loader won’t start” message that support has to untangle.

After delivery, import a conservative preset, test in Deathmatch or a custom lobby, and only then queue ranked. The homepage status note is worth a glance after Riot patches—loading while modules are still marked offline is how people waste keys on patch day.
